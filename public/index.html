Welcome

Your local Guelph electrician for the jobs that keep a home running.

A flickering light. A dead outlet. A breaker that won’t stay on. A ceiling fan, a new fixture, a charger for the car. Small jobs like these are easy to put off, and hard to get a busy contractor to take on.

That’s where I come in. I’m a licensed electrician with over 10 years of residential and industrial experience, and I focus on small residential work. With no fleet of trucks or office full of staff to pay for, I can take on the jobs larger companies skip, and price them fairly.

Big jobs get the attention. Small ones keep your home running.

I serve Guelph, Fergus and the surrounding area, within about 30 minutes. Have a job in mind? Get in touch for a quote.

Electrical Services for Your Home

If it’s electrical and it’s in your home, there’s a good chance I can help, especially with the small and mid-sized jobs that bigger companies tend to pass on. Here are the most common requests.

Troubleshooting & Repairs

Flickering lights, dead outlets, breakers that keep tripping, or switches that run warm. I’ll track down the cause and fix it properly, not just patch the symptom.

Outlets, Switches & Circuits

New outlets where you actually need them, replacements for worn-out ones, GFCI and AFCI protection, and dedicated circuits for appliances like dryers, ranges and freezers.

Lighting & Fans

Pot lights, new fixtures, dimmers, under-cabinet and outdoor lighting, plus ceiling fans and bathroom exhaust fans installed or replaced.

EV Charger Installation

A Level 2 home charger installed cleanly and safely. I’ll check your panel’s capacity first so there are no surprises.

Panel Upgrades & Sub-Panels

Out of breaker space, or still on an older panel? I handle panel repairs and upgrades, and add sub-panels for garages, workshops and renovations.

Smoke & CO Alarms

Hardwired, interconnected smoke and carbon monoxide alarms installed or replaced, so your home stays safe and compliant.

Older Homes & Inspection Fixes

Ungrounded outlets, outdated wiring, or items flagged in a home inspection? I’ll explain what really needs attention, what can wait, and fix what needs fixing.

Garages, Sheds & Outdoor Power

Power for detached garages, workshops and sheds, plus outdoor outlets, security lighting and hot tub hookups.

Don’t see your job on the list? Ask. I’ll tell you straight whether it’s something I can do.

Service area: Guelph, Fergus, Elora, Rockwood, Puslinch, Eden Mills, Acton, Cambridge and surrounding communities, roughly 30 minutes from Guelph or Fergus.

About Me

I’ve been an electrician for over ten years, working on both residential and industrial jobs. Industrial work teaches you to be careful, methodical and safety-first, and to find the real cause of a problem instead of covering it up. I bring that same approach into your home.

Along the way, I noticed a gap. When a homeowner needs a few outlets added, a fixture swapped or a tripping breaker traced, larger electrical companies often can’t make the numbers work. Trucks, staff and offices are expensive to run, and small jobs don’t cover the cost. So homeowners are left with long waits, high minimum charges, or calls that never get returned.

I built my business around those jobs. By keeping my overhead low and my focus narrow, I can take on the small jobs others overlook and charge a fair price for them.

What you can expect from me:

  • A straight answer and a clear price before any work starts
  • Work done to the Ontario Electrical Safety Code, with permits and inspections handled when required
  • A tidy job site and a plain-language explanation of what I did
  • Honest advice, including when something doesn’t need to be done

I’m based in Guelph and proud to work for neighbours across the area. If something electrical at home has been sitting on your to-do list, let’s get it sorted.
