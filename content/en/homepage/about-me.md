I've been an electrician for over ten years, working on both residential and industrial jobs. Industrial work teaches you to be careful, methodical and safety-first, and to find the real cause of a problem instead of covering it up. I bring that same approach into your home.

Along the way, I noticed a gap. When a homeowner needs a few outlets added, a fixture swapped or a tripping breaker traced, larger electrical companies often can't make the numbers work. Trucks, staff and offices are expensive to run, and small jobs don't cover the cost. So homeowners are left with long waits, high minimum charges, or calls that never get returned.

I built my business around those jobs. By keeping my overhead low and my focus narrow, I can take on the small jobs others overlook and charge a fair price for them.

**What you can expect from me:**

- A straight answer and a clear price before any work starts
- Work done to the Ontario Electrical Safety Code, with permits and inspections handled when required
- A tidy job site and a plain-language explanation of what I did
- Honest advice, including when something doesn't need to be done

I'm based in Guelph and proud to work for neighbours across the area. If something electrical at home has been sitting on your to-do list, let's get it sorted.
